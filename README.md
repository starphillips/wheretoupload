## Where To?

**Where To?** was a Flask-based event discovery platform designed to connect Maastricht’s university students and local community with upcoming events, venues, and organisers.

I helped develop the website and worked alongside my friend on the wider business idea, including feature planning, outreach to event organisers, and platform design.

The site included:

- Categorised event listings
- Dedicated event and organiser pages
- Image galleries
- Contact forms
- Threaded comments to encourage community interaction

Although the project was later discontinued due to the challenge of maintaining current event information remotely, it gave me practical experience in full-stack development, product planning, community building, and stakeholder engagement.
